I audited the team's tracking board and made it match reality

The automation that filled our tracking board set each item's status once, when the item was created, and never updated it. The board looked current, but it wasn't.

The situation

In September 2026 I questioned whether the automation behind it kept it accurate. I found it set each status at creation and never refreshed it. The board showed old statuses as if they were current.

A month earlier, in August 2026, I'd found a related problem. When a game was reclassified as an exclusive deal, its original tracking record stayed open forever, so the same delivery showed up twice.

My role

I was the Project Manager and the audit owner. I ran the audit end to end, got sign-off before changing anything, and reviewed my own fixes afterwards.

What I did

  1. I compared every item and sub-item in all nine board groups against its live ticket status. That covered 3,216 items and 16,761 sub-items.
  2. I applied the fixes with sign-off: 699 status changes and 6 moves between groups.
  3. I ran a confidence review of my own fixes. It caught 114 wrong writes of mine, and I reverted every one.
  4. I re-checked live status and promoted 57 more items. I also gave 77 undated items the correct label.
  5. For the duplicates, I traced the root cause to the sync automation and wrote a rule to detect them. I tested that rule against every live record, not a few hand-picked examples.

The result

  • 699 status fixes and 6 group moves are live and verified.
  • 114 of my own mistakes were found and undone.
  • 57 more items promoted after a live re-check, and 77 undated items re-labelled correctly.
  • The duplicate check covered 2,130 records and found 29 duplicates, with 0 false positives.
  • With sign-off, I cancelled 25 duplicate records, plus 37 child records and 3 orphaned tickets.
